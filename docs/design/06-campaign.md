# 06 — Campaign Structure (4 Chapters × 10 Stages)

## 1. Chapter Overview

| Ch | Title | Family | Damage bias | Teaches | Unlocks on completion |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | The Drowned Quarter | Slimes | Enemies mostly magical | Core loop, AoE value, Surge ults, roles | Ch.1-3: Raids menu · Ch.1-5: Gear system · Ch.1-10: Boss menu (Krul rematch) |
| 2 | The Broken Reach | Giants | Enemies mostly physical | Telegraphs, Taunt/DR, Armor Break | Ch.2-3: Guilds · Ch.2-5: Star upgrades UI push · Ch.2-10: Co-op Raid mode |
| 3 | The Sleepless Bastion | Guardians | Mixed, phase-locked | Damage-type flexibility, Strip/Steal, dual-comp | Ch.3-5: Ascension system · Ch.3-10: Hard mode (Ch.1 Hard) |
| 4 | The Singing Graves | Undead | Magical + Decay pressure | Anti-heal, Cleanse cadence, full curriculum | Ch.4-10: Endgame boss rotation + Hard 2–4 |

Design intent: the campaign is a **teaching machine** — each chapter's enemy family forces the class verbs the previous chapter didn't. System unlocks are paced so a new system appears roughly every 20–30 minutes of play in week 1.

## 2. Stage Progression Logic (per chapter, 10 stages)

| Stage | Pattern |
|---|---|
| n-1 → n-3 | Introduce family minions one species at a time; 2 waves/stage |
| n-4 | First elite appears (new verb on display); 3 waves |
| n-5 | **Miniboss A** + minions; drops first-time gear piece |
| n-6 → n-7 | Mixed elites; the chapter's "comp check" mechanic at full strength |
| n-8 | Gauntlet: 3 waves, no heal between (sustain check) |
| n-9 | **Miniboss B** (miniboss A empowered + twist) |
| n-10 | **Chapter boss** (doc 05 §4) — story climax, cutscene, big milestone chest |

- **3-star system:** 1★ clear · 2★ no ally deaths · 3★ clear within 10 rounds. Stars gate chapter completion chests (10/20/30★ cumulative) and unlock stage auto-repeat.
- **Stamina cost:** 6 Aether per stage (ch.1) → 10 (ch.4). First clear refunds 100% (pushing is always "free" — anti-frustration).
- **Difficulty curve target:** stage power requirement grows ~8% per stage inside a chapter, +25% jump between chapters; minibosses +15% over neighbors, chapter boss +30%. Wall stages (by design): 2-7, 3-7, 4-8 — placed where the fix is *tactics or a cheap Common tool*, not raw grind.

## 3. Chapter Identities (mood + content notes for art & narrative)

### Chapter 1 — The Drowned Quarter (Slimes)
A flooded merchant city digesting inside Umbral ooze. Bioluminescent teal against black water; collapsed arcades, floating lanterns. Minions: Sump Slime, Gutter Ooze, Lantern Jelly (magical spitter), Clot (merges). Elites: Mire Colossus, Prism Ooze (reflects one damage type — soft preview of ch.3). Miniboss: The Undertow. Boss: **Krul, the Mother-Mass**.

### Chapter 2 — The Broken Reach (Giants)
A mountain pass shattered by Gatecrushed giants wearing ruins as armor. Granite, rust, low sun through dust. Minions: Rubblekin, Cairn Hurler, Chainback, Ash Ogre. Elites: Wallbreaker, Ironroot Colossus. Miniboss: The Tollkeeper. Boss: **Harrowjaw**.

### Chapter 3 — The Sleepless Bastion (Guardians)
A dead god-fortress whose defense system still runs, defending nothing. Marble and dead gold, geometric light. Minions: Ward Servitor, Lens Sentinel, Gate Custodian, Chorus Node (buff aura — Strip practice). Elites: Null Templar (physical-immune windows), Lattice Warden (magical-immune windows). Miniboss: The Doorwright. Boss: **The Vigil at World's Shoulder**.

### Chapter 4 — The Singing Graves (Undead)
Endless terraced necropolis where the dead sing the Gate's song. Bone-white terraces under a green aurora. Minions: Gravechoir Thrall, Bonewrought, Lament Wisp, Carrion Sexton (healer — anti-heal practice). Elites: Requiem Knight, Tidebound Mass (resurrects once). Miniboss: The Choirmaster. Boss: **Annor-Vhol, the Gravetide Chorus**.

## 4. Hard Mode (launch+0, gated behind 3-10 / 4-10)

Same stages, enemies +80% stats, +1 modifier per stage (extra elite, active mutator, or debuff aura). Drops: Ascension materials + higher-tier gear. 3 entries/stage/day. Hard mode exists so campaign stages stay farm-relevant and chapter art amortizes.

## 5. Collection Hook

Each chapter has a **Codex**: enemy cards (art from the enemy card pipeline, doc 11 §7) unlocked by kills, granting one-time premium currency per completed family page. Cheap content, real retention: completionists re-run chapters, and Higgsfield enemy art gets a second life as collectibles.
